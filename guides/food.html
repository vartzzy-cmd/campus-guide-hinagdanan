Eating Well on a Student Budget

  • Your first year is busy: new classes, new friends, and long days on campus. It easy to skip meals or spend your whole allowance on milktea. Eating regular, simple meals helps you focus in class, and you do not need a bid budget to do it.

    • Where to Eat Near Campus

  • You have more options than you think. here are the useal go-to spots:
    1. The school canteen for quick rice meals between classes
    2. Carinderias outside the gate for cheap, filling ulam and rice
    3. Your own baob from the home, which is the cheapest choice of all
    4. Convenience stores for emergency snacks, not for every mail

    How to Plan Your Weekly food Budget

  • A late planning on sunday saves you from Being broke by thursday.
    1. Check how much allowance you have for the whole week.
    2. Set aside money for fare and school needs first.
    3. Divide What is Left by number of dchool days.
    4. Pack baon on at least two days to stretch your budget.
    5. Keep a small amount for emergencies. like a suprise project.

    Quiclk Tips

  • Always bring a refillable eater bottle. water station on campus are free, and and you will save a lot compared to buying bottled drinks every day.
